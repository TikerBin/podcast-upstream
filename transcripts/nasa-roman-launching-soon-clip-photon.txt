Far away from us, around distant stars, scientists have already found thousands of these exoplanets. Roman will look toward the center of our own Milky Way. Where it can see literally hundreds of thousands of stars in a single image at once. We know that most stars have planets. Which means Roman will detect thousands more exoplanets out there. With a cutting-edge piece of technology, Roman will see far more detail of some planets than we've ever seen before. These planets are so incredibly faint that you get one photon, one particle of light. Arriving at our camera from one of those planets once every few seconds or maybe even once every minute. So we are getting one little smudge to try to tease out what the atmosphere might be made of.
